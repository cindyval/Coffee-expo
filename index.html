About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is a platform bringing together coffee farmers, exporters, processors, researchers and industry professionals to discuss the future of Uganda's coffee industry.

The event will showcase innovations in coffee production, processing, marketing and international trade while creating opportunities for networking and business partnerships.
